# AR digital layout placement — 2026-09-28

## Behavior under review

`Archive + Place Here` preserves the digital world and creates a new room anchor at the wearer's current floor position and facing. The prior VR composition therefore moves and turns as one unit. Measured room outlines describe the physical scan, not the fit of the digital scene. This candidate adds explicit in-headset **World → Move / Turn Digital Layout** controls after a persistent room anchor is tracked. Each tap moves the digital root 0.25 m in its current facing or turns it 15° around the center of its web-floor objects. Object IDs, authored local transforms, game and Citizens data do not change.

The root offset is browser-owned and stored only with the matching persistent room anchor. It is composed with the tracked anchor before `webFloorPose` is published to the existing room-placement guards. Every nudge invalidates the selected destination and tracking epoch, clears measured-room confirmation, and requires **I Checked Scene Clearance** before the separate measured-outline confirmation. An active Agent turn, pending receipt, or world exchange blocks adjustment. A browser checkpoint or world archive replacement during AR applies the same invalidation even when its offset matches, because different objects can occupy different physical space. Room recovery exports the old offset with its old anchor and starts the new anchor at zero.

The clearance review is the wearer's observation. Moving a pre-existing layout does not automatically prove that every object still fits a measured support or clears a wall, TV, or furniture. PC world checkpoints remain renderer-neutral and do not claim physical placement.

The PC-local WebAuthoring generator also registers the exact local bounds of its GLB geometry. A registered generated model can then pass the same measured-support footprint guard as other bounded assets. Rebuilding an identical recipe repairs an older same-ID catalog entry that lacked bounds; the earlier failed placement receipt remains failed.

A measured surface spawn stores its pose in the persistent web-floor scene. A tracked upright room pose can serialize the equivalent yaw as XYZ Euler x/z near 180°, which prevents a later room-constrained move from accepting the stored rotation. The source now normalizes a pose within 0.25° of upright to its equivalent YXZ yaw, then checks that stored pose against the measured support and other planes before insertion. A genuinely tilted support retains its full rotation. The live pedestal was placed before this normalization and received an explicit upright yaw on its successful move; the new automatic normalization is covered by source tests.
