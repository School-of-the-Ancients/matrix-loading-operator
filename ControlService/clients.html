Client connections

Allow an application on this PC to read the connected scene and suggest changes. Every change still needs your review and Apply here. Pairing does not share camera images or authorize installation of content.

Proposals expire after two minutes and whenever the scene or selection changes. Ask the client for a new proposal after reviewing the current scene.

Pair a local client

The service must have a token of at least 24 characters. It stays in this page's memory. Client tokens have narrower permissions; do not give the service token to clients.

Open the intended runtime before pairing. Give the temporary code only to the client you want to connect.
