# Quest acceptance

Current v1.2 requirements and results are in the [single H01–H10 acceptance record](https://github.com/School-of-the-Ancients/matrix-loading-operator/blob/main/Validation/V1.2-Headset-Acceptance.md). Use its candidate identities, scope exclusions, remaining checks and evidence-reuse rules. Record VR and AR separately and preserve the wearer's existing world, site data and room origin.

The [earlier Quest record](https://github.com/School-of-the-Ancients/matrix-loading-operator/blob/60ab4599752147f2e590293208bee4692965a524/WebRuntime/QUEST3_ACCEPTANCE.md) preserves older mode-specific runs and broader room checks. It is historical evidence, not a second current release checklist. Controls are documented in the [runtime guide](README.md#quest-controls-faq).
