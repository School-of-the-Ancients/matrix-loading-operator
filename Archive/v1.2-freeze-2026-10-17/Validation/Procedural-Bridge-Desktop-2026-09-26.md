Its `work/bridge-acceptance-state` scene and asset directories were separate from the user's services and worlds. The browser advertised `matrix-web` / `threejs-webxr` in desktop presentation. Typed requests were queued through `/api/command`; each edit below was accepted by the connected `MatrixBridge` and checked against a matching `/api/state` receipt and live scene. The [compact trace](procedural-bridge-browser.json) records IDs, parameters, transforms and receipts; the [browser screenshot](bridge-acceptance-browser.png) shows the rendered revised bridge. The browser reported zero console errors or warnings after the final built-page reload.
