## Independent ML, personal input and geography

The ML project proceeds on its own schedule and under its course policy. Any Matrix/Civilization integration starts with labeled synthetic or replayed observations; independently produced classifier outputs and live phone input are separate later steps.

Manfred/Demerzel owns private human context. Activity predictions and geographic fixes need their own sources, times, uncertainty and consent; neither establishes intention or exact XR alignment. Share only permitted observations, with stop/revoke and retention behavior covering derived resident memory. Do not put real routes, private account history or room scans in public fixtures.

Personal ChatGPT account memory, API-provider memory and simulated resident memory are different sources; an integration must demonstrate a supported, authorized path before promising access.

Graph-algorithm work can proceed against existing navigation fixtures independently of the School integration. Keep deterministic path execution separate from model goal selection, and distinguish route finding from network-design experiments.
